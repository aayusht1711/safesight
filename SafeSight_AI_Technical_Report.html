1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

SafeSight AI is an asynchronous Trust & Safety platform built to moderate multimodal content in real time while concurrently performing deep digital media forensics. Modern platforms face two fundamental challenges:

1. The Moderation Cost & Latency Dilemma

Simple keyword blocklists fail against adversarial bypasses (leet-speak, homoglyphs). Conversely, routing every post to a monolithic Vision-Language Model (VLM) takes 3–5 seconds and causes unsustainable cloud GPU compute costs.

2. The Deepfake & Tampering Threat

Modern online violations extend beyond harmful text into localized AI inpainting, deepfake face-swapping, and voice-cloned audio that bypass standard text-only filters.

The SafeSight AI Solution: A Cascaded Two-Tier Architecture where an ultra-fast Tier-1 gate (<15ms) handles >85% of traffic, reserving the deep Vision-Language Model (Tier-2 Qwen2.5) only for complex borderline cases, while an integrated 4-modality digital forensics suite detects synthetic generation and localized tampering.

3. Engine Deep-Dive & Key Technical Innovations

Layer 0: Adversarial Evasion Shield

  • Leet-Speak Normalization: Translates obfuscated characters (e.g. p0is0n → poison, d3@th → death, h@t3 → hate).
  • Homoglyph Mapping: Maps lookalike Cyrillic/Greek characters to standard Latin equivalents via Unicode NFKC.
  • Zero-Width Stripping: Removes invisible joiners and non-printable evasion markers.
  • Perceptual dHash: Instant 64-bit image hashing against known-banned media (<2ms, 0 GPU load).

Layer 1: Tier-1 Fast Gate (<15ms)

  • Short-Circuit Routing: Analyzes text and image embeddings against calibrated safe vs unsafe vector anchors.
  • High-Throughput: High-confidence safe (>0.82) or clear violations (>0.82) resolve immediately on CPU.
  • Cost Optimization: Absorbs 85–90% of platform traffic, slashing server costs by ~85%.

Layer 2: Tier-2 Explainable VLM

  • Bimodal Synergy Principle: Evaluates how text and visual context interact (e.g. aggressive words paired with a funny meme template are approved as harmless hyperbole).
  • Deterministic JSON: Generates strict Pydantic schemas with policy codes (e.g. MLC_H1.1) and three-part reasoning.

Layer 4: Caching & Fail-Safe Resilience

  • Deterministic SHA-256: Multi-modal hashing enables sub-millisecond repeated lookups.
  • Zero-Crash Fallback: If ML backends are warming up, automatically falls back to HUMAN_AUDIT_REQUIRED without 500 errors.
